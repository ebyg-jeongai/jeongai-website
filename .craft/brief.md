## Audience

As in the demo brief [DEMO]: owners and operators of businesses running on generic software
and patched automations, who must understand within thirty seconds "that we're just
different" and see "that we're capable of building something for them" [USER]. Mostly on
phones; desktop must also look good [USER]. Second audience: buyers of the three digital
products, who arrive at checkout pages from the home page or from search, and return to the
product apps after purchase with a license key [REPO]. Third: existing buyers who need the
unlock flow to work today [REPO inventory].

## Constraints

Design [BG, USER]: as in the demo brief. Cormorant Garamond and Inter from Google Fonts,
palette and proportion rule, mark rules, voice rules, craft ban list, mobile first, one
breakpoint, body contrast 4.5:1, keyboard operable, reduced motion respected.

Engineering [REPO inventory, CLAUDE.md]:
- Plain HTML, CSS and JS. No build tools. Azure Static Web Apps deploys develop to staging
  and master to production. Work happens on a feature branch, then develop. Nothing is
  pushed or merged without the owner saying so.
- Preserve: GA4 G-CX2B49MG98 on every public page with events generate_lead, sign_up,
  begin_checkout (data-product), purchase; Web3Forms endpoint and access key for the audit
