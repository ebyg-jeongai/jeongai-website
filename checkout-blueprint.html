Common questions

Do I need to buy the Starter Kit first?
No. The Blueprint includes everything from the Starter Kit. You get full access to all Starter Kit content plus the Blueprint frameworks, templates and tools.
What is the difference between the Starter Kit and the Blueprint?
The Starter Kit teaches you what tools to use and gives you prompts and scenarios. The Blueprint teaches you how to build complete automation systems, with frameworks, workflow templates, an interactive system builder and implementation checklists.
How does the system builder work?
The system builder is an interactive drag-and-drop canvas where you design your automation architecture. Pick nodes from the palette (triggers, actions, AI steps, integrations) and connect them to map out your workflows visually.

No. The Blueprint is a one-time purchase of $79. You get lifetime access to all content and future updates.

Yes. When you are ready for the complete package, you can move to the Small Business AI Toolkit, which includes everything in the Blueprint plus additional playbooks, industry workflows, and a credit toward Jeong AI implementation.
